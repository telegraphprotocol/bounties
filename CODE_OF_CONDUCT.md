# Code of Conduct

Be useful, specific and respectful.

- Keep criticism focused on the work.
- Submit relevant contributions and represent evidence and completion accurately.
- Use the private reporting channel for security-sensitive information.
- Discuss reward decisions professionally.
- Maintainers may close low-signal or abusive contributions that create more work than value.

The standard is simple: **does this help the network become more useful?**

## Scope

This applies to Issues, pull requests, comments, bounty commands and any other space run under this repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close Issues, release claims, lock conversations, and block users who breach this code. Repeated or serious breaches, including fabricated proof, may lead to a permanent ban. Decisions on claims and rewards follow [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **info@telegraphprotocol.com** with the subject `[CONDUCT]`. Include links to the relevant Issue or comment. Reports are handled in confidence, and a maintainer who is a party to the concern will not decide it.
