# Demand → contribution → usable capability

This repository is Telegraph's **public contribution queue**. Private Anjoman jobs, commercial records, payments and live protocol state remain in their respective authorized systems. H2 work uses `source:h2`; customer, mainnet, application, release and ongoing needs share the same reviewed queue.

## Two different requests

- **Private customer/Anjoman job:** owned by the requesting account. Its prompt, documents, contact details, budget, files, provider or measurement diagnostics and job state stay in authorized systems. The customer may save a blocked request and opt into notification.
- **Public opportunity:** a maintainer-approved, sanitized, reusable gap with clear public acceptance criteria. It may attract a Provider or contributor while preserving customer and evaluation confidentiality. The Issue states its funding status.

## Qualify and publish manually

1. Verify the need: a buyer-approved requirement, failed authorized App or job, current network gap, or important verifiable external release. Check whether an existing Intent or service already covers it. Create an Intent only for a reusable intelligence primitive.
2. Choose a route. Use `opportunity:provider` for commercial recruitment of an established independent service. Use a normal Provider adapter, Evaluator, integration, tooling, reliability, security or protocol bounty for reusable implementation work. Route sensitive security and evaluation findings through the private security process.
3. Obtain authorization for every fact that could identify a customer or reveal sensitive telemetry. Publish the minimum useful description. Keep raw prompts, documents, identifiable request and response examples, private budgets, exploitable weaknesses, hidden evaluator cases, per-Intent diagnostic deficits and private monitoring thresholds in their authorized systems. Label synthetic examples clearly.
4. Create a `status:triage` Issue. Search for duplicates, verify the source before applying a `source:*` label, and specify scope, public acceptance, accountable reviewer and reward status. The `source:customer` label identifies the origin of an approved need; it makes no statement about customer identity or payment.
5. Use `/open` for approved, reproducible bounty work. Commercial Provider opportunities may be `status:open` for outreach and continue through the separate commercial process.
6. After delivery, reproduce the change and verify Intent registration, eligible Provider or Evaluator state, routing, payment and **the original authorized App or job**. Production verification determines whether a capability is available. Notify the account through its own system and obtain fresh authorization before paid or side-effectful work.

## Privacy-preserving integration boundary

An authorized product may send a sanitized candidate to its private operations queue: an opaque correlation token, broad capability or task category, reusable success criteria, an authorized demand band when needed, consent record and review status. Operators can then use the existing Issue Forms to publish approved work. This repository has no ingestion API and receives no live customer payloads or internal node and evaluation measurements. Keep any correlation token that could identify a job in the private queue.

The **authoritative production system** verifies availability and eligibility before any capability notification. The original customer then decides whether to retry, and repository events never authorize charges or restart dormant jobs.

## Public proof

After verification, a customer may approve an Explorer outcome or other public result that states what became available, the version and time, what was verified, and a supported next action (Use, Compete, Evaluate, Solve). Private jobs and results remain private by default. Mark historical outcomes as historical, distinguish internal or bootstrapped traffic from external paid usage, and support earnings, savings, ranking and funded-demand claims with evidence.

## Priority and responsibility

Prioritize authorized production blockers, then credible reusable application gaps and important time-sensitive market coverage. Maintainers verify and publish approved work; engineering validates production readiness; commercial staff own buyer permission and Provider recruitment; Finance or the CEO approves reward commitments. GitHub coordinates the contribution process, while those systems and roles remain authoritative for their decisions.
